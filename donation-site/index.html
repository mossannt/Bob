Questions, answered

Good to know.

Where does my donation go?+

Support helps cover development tools, hosting, creative assets, testing, and the time required to keep building and sharing new work.

Is there a minimum amount?+

Not at all. Give only what feels comfortable. A kind message or sharing the project is also valuable support.

Can I support without donating?+

Yes. Try the project, report an issue, share it with someone who might enjoy it, or send a note of encouragement.
