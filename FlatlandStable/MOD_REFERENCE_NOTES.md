# Reference notes for original Godot adaptation

Source video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q-EgDU_U4ZE

The referenced video was analyzed as the Dream Shift Minecraft mod concept. Its central loop is exploration in a bright natural hub world, discovering thin glowing vertical slits or anomalies, entering surreal liminal dimensions, completing simple environmental objectives or surviving hazards, then returning to the main world. Dream spaces described include sterile infinite office or mall corridors, a snowy church in a dark void, an endless green field with an isolated house, a redwood forest with a floating red house, a flower train in a red poppy field, a terraced flooded pit, a windmill city, and a flooded office.

Relevant mechanics for an original mobile Godot adaptation include health and hunger, death/respawn with possible inventory loss, a bottom hotbar and full inventory, resource gathering, simple crafting, buildable structures, passive and hostile creatures, and unsettling dream entities. Visual direction is bright and natural in the hub, contrasted with surreal scale, fog, voids, impossible geometry, and glowing slit portals in dream spaces. Audio direction includes nature ambience in the hub and wind, echoing footsteps, bells, or low-frequency hums in liminal spaces.

The adaptation must be original and should not copy copyrighted Minecraft assets, names, or exact maps. The first milestone can focus on the bright green survival hub, a glowing slit portal, mobile movement and interaction, a hotbar/inventory, and one original liminal room.
